Proof before profit

Anyone can show a great backtest.

Edge Registry is a public register for trading strategies. You commit to the rules before the results exist. We test the strategy against random data, then judge it on real trades — and the verdict stays public, even when it fails.

The problem with trading track records

Most bots, EAs and signal services look profitable for the same reason: you only see the winners.

Hidden attempts

Run fifty accounts, show the best one. Run ten thousand backtests, sell the best one. The buyer never sees the other 9,999.

Curve-fitting

Tune a strategy long enough and it will fit the past perfectly — including the noise that will never repeat.

Moving goalposts

“It needs more time”, “that was a bad month”. Without rules fixed in advance, no result can ever count against a strategy.

How it works

Borrowed from medicine, where trials are registered before they start so that nobody can bury the bad results.

1 · Register

Fix the rules first

Strategy, markets, account and the pass rule — for example “50 independent trades, positive average, at least 2 standard errors above zero”. Timestamped on the Bitcoin blockchain.

2 · Noise test

Beat random data

Optional: we run your strategy on the same prices shuffled into random order. If it looks just as good there, the backtest proves nothing.

3 · Measure

Real trades only

Read-only connection to your broker (MetaTrader, Capital.com, OANDA). Trades on the same day, direction and asset class count as one bet.

4 · Verdict

Public, either way

Passed or failed, by the rule you set. Early stops only for clear losses, never for lucky streaks. Every entry stays in the registry.

We built this because our own bot fooled us

We bred trading strategies for 12,000 generations — millions of them — on seven years of prices. The best ones looked spectacular in the backtest.

Then we ran the same search on the same prices in random order, where no strategy can possibly have an edge. It found strategies that looked just as good. Our backtests had been measuring luck.

So we froze 201 strategies and fixed the rules for judging them before a single trade, and we tested a textbook trend-following strategy the same way: the trend was real, but on CFDs the overnight fees ate all of it. None of that would have shown up in a backtest screenshot.

Our own experiment will be entry #1 in the registry — whatever its verdict.

Questions

Do I have to share my strategy's code?

No. The registry only needs the rules you commit to and read-only access to the account. The noise test is optional and needs the code or the strategy's signals.

Is a passed verdict a guarantee of future profit?

No. It is evidence that a strategy did better than chance under rules fixed in advance. Markets change; past results never guarantee future ones.

Is this financial advice?

No. We do not recommend, sell or trade any strategy. We measure and publish.

What does it cost?

Looking up the registry is free. Pricing for registering strategies will be set with the first users on the waitlist.
