An idea in testing

Anyone can show a great backtest.
Few can show they fixed the rules first.

Edge Registry is an idea for a public register of trading strategies: the rules are fixed before the results exist, the strategy is tested against shuffled prices, and it is judged on forward trades, with the verdict staying public even when it fails. We are trying it on our own bot first: v2 runs in the open, on a demo account.

The problem with trading track records

Most bots, EAs and signal services look profitable for the same reason: you only see the winners.

Hidden attempts

Run fifty accounts, show the best one. Run ten thousand backtests, show the best one. Nobody sees the other 9,999.

Curve-fitting

Tune a strategy long enough and it will fit the past perfectly, including the noise that will never repeat.

Moving goalposts

“It needs more time”, “that was a bad month”. Without rules fixed in advance, no result can ever count against a strategy.

The idea in four steps

Borrowed from medicine, where trials are registered before they start so that nobody can bury the bad results.

1 · Register

Fix the rules first

Strategy, markets and the pass rule (for example “50 independent events, positive average, t of at least 2.2”), written down and timestamped before trading starts.

2 · Noise test

Beat shuffled prices

Run the same strategy search on the same prices in random order. If it looks just as good there, the backtest proves nothing.

3 · Measure

Forward trades only

Count only trades opened after registration. Trades on the same day, in the same direction and asset class count as one event.

4 · Verdict

Public, either way

Passed or failed, by the rule set in advance. Early stops only for clear losses, never for lucky streaks. No verdict by the deadline counts as failed.

Why: our own bot fooled us

We bred trading strategies for 12,000 generations, millions of them, on seven years of prices. The best ones looked spectacular in the backtest.

Then we ran the same search on the same prices in random order, where the order of prices carries no pattern. It found strategies that looked even better. Our backtests could not tell skill from luck.

So we froze 201 strategies and fixed the rules for judging them before a single trade. They now trade on a demo account with play money until 12 December 2026. We also tested a textbook trend-following strategy the same way: the trend was real, but on CFDs the overnight fees ate all of it.

We will publish the verdict of our own test here, whatever it is.

Questions

No. It is a test project. Nothing is sold or offered here.

No. The bot and the testing tools stay private. We share the method and the results, not the code.

Nothing here promises any profit. Our own backtests could not be told apart from luck, which is the reason this project exists. Even a passed test would only be evidence of better-than-chance results under rules fixed in advance, not a guarantee.
